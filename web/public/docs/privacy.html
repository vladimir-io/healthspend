Privacy

No accounts. No advertising profiles. Built for hospital price transparency.

What we collect

Optional anonymous product telemetry (RUM) may record:

  • Page path (no URL hash or query string)
  • Event type (page view, search timing, dispute funnel step)
  • Coarse campaign tags if you arrived via a shared link (utm_*)
  • Referrer hostname only (not the full URL)

We do not intentionally collect procedure codes, hospital names, emails, or free-text search queries in telemetry.

What we do not do

  • No user accounts or login
  • No sale of personal data
  • No third-party ad trackers in the core app

Where data lives

Telemetry counters are stored in Cloudflare KV for operational reliability. Price data is loaded from a public Hugging Face dataset into your browser.

Legal

Healthspend is for transparency research and education — not legal, medical, or billing advice. See the in-app legal disclosure and LEGAL.md.
